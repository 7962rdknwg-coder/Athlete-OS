Competitions

A competitions trigger Green → Amber → Red. B competitions are trained through and may replace a HIGH exposure. Where practical, place a scheduled deload after a B competition.

GREEN

Build

Test → 5 progressive weeks → deload. Higher training volume and physical development.

AMBER

Sharpen

<6 weeks from an A competition. Maintain strength, reduce unnecessary volume, increase specificity and speed/power quality.

RED

Taper

Final 7–10 days. Reduce fatigue, retain small doses of speed/intensity, then perform.

Programming rules

Z2
Same duration + same HR zone → aim to cover more distance. Add time only if progress genuinely plateaus.
Sprints
Same distance/setup → faster time. Do not add volume just to create overload.
Resisted sprints
Get faster at a given resistance before adding load. Timing gates decide whether quality is maintained.
Strength
Progress through load/performance. Keep the exercise structure stable enough to compare.
High / Low
Classify the total day by stress, not just individual exercises. Protect low days from turning into accidental high days.
